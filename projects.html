01 / SAAS PRODUCT · UNDER DEVELOPMENT
SPRINGTRUSTCOMPLIANCE HUB
SPRINGTRUST COMPLIANCE HUB

Compliance support designed for smaller organisations.

SpringTrust is an AI-powered governance, risk, compliance, policy and assurance SaaS platform under development for UK SMEs, charities and CICs.

Smaller organisations often struggle to work out which requirements apply to them, keep policies and procedures current, understand the impact of regulatory changes and show that their controls are working. SpringTrust is being designed around those everyday challenges, rather than assuming every organisation has a dedicated compliance department.

Its guiding principle is simple: technology should help professionals make better decisions, not replace accountable human judgement.

02 / GRANTMO · PILOT STAGE

Helping community organisations manage grants with greater clarity.

GrantMo is a grant-management platform for charities and CICs that has progressed to its pilot stage.

Its purpose is to reduce confusion around grant opportunities and applications and support clearer records, responsibilities and governance across the grant lifecycle. My contribution is focused on digital trust, policy, compliance and security considerations that matter when organisations manage sensitive operational information.

The pilot phase is intended to test workflows, collect user feedback and inform subsequent product improvements. We are not presenting pilot activity as evidence of market-wide adoption or independently measured customer outcomes.

03 / INFORMATION SECURITY MANAGEMENT · 2026

GMBTE — ISMS development and security governance.

Applied information security management principles in an organisational setting by developing structured governance resources aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

My work included the organisational context, ISMS scope, information security objectives, policy suite, roles and responsibilities, risk assessment methodology, risk register, risk treatment plan, Statement of Applicability and information asset register.

I also worked on security governance relating to vulnerability review and tracking security work through GitHub and Jira processes. The value of this work lies in translating standards into explicit responsibilities, repeatable processes and decisions that organisations can act upon.

The case study summarises my contributions without sharing confidential files or claiming independently certified ISO/IEC 27001 compliance.

Selected workstreams
Management structure

ISMS scope, context, policies, objectives and accountability.

Risk-based planning

Risk methodology, assessment, treatment and statement of applicability.

Technical assurance

Vulnerability review, access-control considerations and tracked remediation.

Evidence discipline

Asset inventory, versioned governance documents and review processes.

04 / CAPSTONE PROJECT · JULY 2026

Inegben Academy — Corporate Compliance & AI Governance Audit.

Led a multidisciplinary GRC capstone team during the final phase of Inegben Academy's Global GRC Training & Certification Programme, completed in August 2026.

The team undertook a structured control-gap and risk assessment exercise covering identity and access management, network and endpoint security, data protection, logging and monitoring, business continuity, third-party risk and AI governance.

We developed a prioritised remediation roadmap informed by ISO/IEC 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 22301. As team lead, my work brought together assessment planning, technical and governance analysis, reporting and recommendations for a coherent improvement pathway.

This description is deliberately anonymised. The underlying report is confidential and subject to an NDA; client identity, individual findings, ratings and internal costings are not published.
