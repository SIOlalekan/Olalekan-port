Brief Background

Sodeeq Ipadeola Olalekan is a Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) professional with a unique blend of expertise across education, IT, cybersecurity, and regulatory compliance. With over eight years of experience in IT and a strong academic foundation in education, he brings a well-rounded perspective to managing risk, ensuring compliance, and strengthening organisational resilience.

His career reflects a deliberate transition from technical operations into governance and assurance, driven by a passion for aligning people, processes, and technology with regulatory and ethical standards.

Value He Brings

Sodeeq offers a rare combination of technical knowledge, regulatory awareness, and human-centred insight. He helps organisations:
