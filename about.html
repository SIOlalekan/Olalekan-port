THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

From technical foundations to digital trust leadership.

My journey has been shaped by education, hands-on technology experience and a growing commitment to helping organisations use digital systems responsibly.

MY STORY

A professional journey built through experience and curiosity.

The deeper lesson from my journey: reliable technology depends on clear processes, responsible decisions and the people who use it.

I am Sodeeq Ipadeola Olalekan, a UK-based digital trust, cybersecurity governance and compliance professional. My approach is rooted in an understanding that technology is most useful when the people, processes and decisions around it are equally well considered.

My first substantial experience came through IT operations, infrastructure and security controls at Westmidlands CBT. Working closely with operational systems taught me the importance of reliability, access control, information handling and practical problem-solving. In a sessional role at the British Council, I also developed experience in examination compliance, confidentiality, incident escalation and procedural discipline.

My academic background in education has been an important part of this development. A Bachelor of Education from the University of Ibadan and an MA from the University of Greater Manchester strengthened my ability to research, think systematically, work with people and communicate complex ideas. I continue to draw on these skills in cybersecurity awareness, policy development and professional training.

As my interests developed, I moved into compliance monitoring and assurance at INHAF Ltd, before taking on the role of Head of Digital Trust, Policy & Compliance at Greater Manchester Black Tech Expo (GMBTE). There, I contributed to information security management, ISO/IEC 27001-aligned governance documentation, risk processes and security improvement work.

Currently, as Director and Head of Digital Trust, Policy & Compliance at Spring Together CIC, I bring these experiences together through responsible technology initiatives, digital inclusion and governance work. My activities also include development contributions to SpringTrust Compliance Hub and GrantMo, research, professional volunteering and public knowledge-sharing.

Across these experiences, the same principle has become increasingly important to me: governance should enable informed decisions and meaningful innovation, rather than become a barrier to progress.
