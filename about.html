THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Building confidence in a digital world.

I am Sodeeq Ipadeola Olalekan, a UK-based digital trust, cybersecurity governance and GRC professional with a foundation in IT operations and infrastructure.

From technical support and security controls to risk management, governance and policy development, my experience has shaped a practical approach to digital trust: good governance should help technology serve people, not become a barrier to progress.

At Spring Together CIC, I contribute to the organisation's digital governance, assurance, responsible technology initiatives and community-focused programmes. My work also includes developing GRC tooling through SpringTrust Compliance Hub and contributing to the evolution of GrantMo.

I contribute to research and professional knowledge-sharing on digital trust, emerging technology and responsible AI, including published academic work and invited speaking.

“Governance works best when it is evidence-led, human-centred and genuinely useful.”
