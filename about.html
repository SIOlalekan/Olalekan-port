The Journey

The Path to Assurance.

A deliberate, strategic evolution from education and frontline support into high-level cybersecurity governance.

1. The Education Foundation (Empathy & Human Risk)

Starting in education (MA & BEd) equipped me with a profound understanding of safeguarding, data privacy (GDPR), and the critical human element of compliance. This phase built my ability to translate complex rules into workable school policies.

2. The IT Operations Phase (Technical Reality)

Transitioning to frontline IT Support provided eight years of hard, hands-on experience dealing with networks, identity management (IAM), systems administration, and disaster recovery. I learned exactly how technical controls eventually fail, and why.

3. The Cybersecurity & GRC Ascension (Strategic Governance)

Armed with human empathy and technical reality, I upskilled into formal cybersecurity (ISC2, Google) and stepped up as a Compliance & Assurance Analyst. Today, I don't just secure systems—I govern them, ensuring regulatory strictness aligns flawlessly with organizational flow.
