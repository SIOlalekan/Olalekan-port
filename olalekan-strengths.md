# Olalekan's Strengths & Value Proposition

## 1. Critical vs Non-Critical Infrastructure
### Education
- **Critical:** Student data systems (MIS), safeguarding records, Exam systems, Learning platforms (LMS/VLE), Safeguarding compliance.
- **Angle:** Strong fit for safeguarding compliance, data protection (GDPR), and audit roles.

### IT
- **Critical:** Networks, servers, cloud systems, IAM, Backup/DR, Security monitoring.
- **Angle:** Understands IT operations + governance → ideal for IT risk, controls, audits, ISO 27001.

### Consultancy
- **Critical:** Client data handling, Regulatory frameworks, Risk assessments.
- **Angle:** Can translate technical risk into business language (rare and valuable).

### Support (Care/Admin)
- **Critical:** Care records, CQC / safeguarding rules, Incident reporting.
- **Angle:** Understands frontline operations → real-world risk assessments, not just theory.

### Cybersecurity
- **Critical:** Threat detection, Data protection, IAM, Vulnerabilty management.
- **Angle:** Not just technical — understands people, behavior, and policy.

## 2. Value Chain (Where Olalekan Fits Best)
1. **Policy & Governance:** GRC Analyst, Compliance & Assurance, Policy development. (Strong Fit)
2. **Risk Identification:** Mix of education + IT helps identify risks others miss.
3. **Control Design & Implementation:** Bridge between technical controls (IT/Cyber) and human controls (Education/Safeguarding).
4. **Operations:** Real understanding of how policies actually work on the ground due to support/admin background.
5. **Monitoring & Assurance:** Compliance & Assurance Analyst strictly aligns here.

## 3. High-Value Paths
**Best Niche:** Education + Cybersecurity + Compliance (e.g., universities, edtech).
**Unique Advantage:** 
Most people are either Technical OR Compliance. 
Olalekan is Education-aware, IT-experienced, Cybersecurity-trained, and working in compliance. This combination is highly rare.
